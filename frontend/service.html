✨ Core Features

Everything you need to manage your wholesale business

  • 📦 Product Management Add and organize inventory with ease. Track stock levels, set low-stock alerts, and generate comprehensive inventory reports. Manage product details, prices, and quantities all in one place.
  • 🛒 Order Processing Handle sales orders quickly and efficiently. Create orders with automatic inventory reduction, track order status from pending to delivered, and manage multiple products per order seamlessly.
  • 🚚 Shipping Tools Assign and track deliveries in real-time. Generate unique tracking numbers, update shipment status, monitor current locations, and set estimated delivery dates. Keep customers informed every step of the way.
  • 📄 Invoice Generator Automatically create and send clean, professional invoices. Generate detailed invoices with company branding, itemized product lists, totals, and print-ready formats. Streamline your billing process.
  • 👥 Customer Manager View purchase history and contact details in one comprehensive dashboard. Track customer statistics including total orders, spending, average order value, and pending orders. Build stronger customer relationships.
  • 💳 Payment Processing Accept multiple payment methods including PayPal, M-PESA, cash, bank transfers, and credit/debit cards. Link payments to orders, track payment status, and maintain complete payment history.
  • 📊 Sales Reports Get insights into your business with comprehensive sales reports. View total sales, order counts, best-selling products, top customers, and more. Make data-driven decisions.
  • 📈 Inventory Analytics Monitor inventory value, low stock alerts, out-of-stock items, and total inventory worth. Get real-time insights into your stock levels and make informed purchasing decisions.
